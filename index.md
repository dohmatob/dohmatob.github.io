<p>
Welcome to my blog! I am an Associate Professor at 
<a href="https://www.concordia.ca/faculty/elvis-dohmatob.html">Concordia University</a> 
and an affiliate faculty member at the 
<a href="https://mila.quebec/fr/annuaire/elvis-dohmatob">Mila Quebec AI Institute</a>.

Previously, I was a researcher at 
<a href="https://ai.meta.com/">FAIR (Meta)</a>, 
<a href="https://www.criteo.com/">Criteo</a>, 
and <a href="https://www.inria.fr/">INRIA</a> (Parietal team).
</p>

<p>
My research interests include: neural scaling laws, optimization, 
ML phenomena (grokking, adversarial examples, model collapse), 
random matrix theory / free probability, privacy and fairness in machine learning, 
as well as theoretical aspects of deep learning more broadly.
</p>
